# Amazon E-Commerce Sales Analytics

## Executive Summary
Analysis was conducted on 100,000 Amazon orders to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, payment preferences, and product performance.

## Key Findings

### Product Performance
Top Revenue Generating Products:
1. Memory Card 128GB - $1.94M
2. LED Desk Lamp - $1.92M
3. Mechanical Keyboard - $1.91M
4. Electric Kettle - $1.91M
5. Smartwatch - $1.90M

### Category Analysis
Top Categories by Revenue:
1. Electronics - $15.58M
2. Sports & Outdoors - $15.35M
3. Books - $15.26M
4. Clothing - $15.25M
5. Toys & Games - $15.22M

### Geographic Analysis
Top Revenue Cities:
1. Charlotte - $4.75M
2. Dallas - $4.73M
3. San Jose - $4.67M
4. Seattle - $4.66M
5. Philadelphia - $4.65M

### Brand Performance
Top Revenue Brand:
- CoreTech - $9.34M

### Payment Insights
Most Used Payment Method:
- Credit Card (35,038 orders)

Second Most Used:
- Debit Card (20,024 orders)

### Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing efforts on Electronics products.
- Strengthen inventory planning for top-selling products.
- Expand promotions in Charlotte and Dallas markets.
- Enhance Credit Card offers and loyalty programs.
